Gold labels

Support task. Read the CLAIM and the PAGE TEXT. Use only the page text, not what you know. supports: the page states the key facts, or they follow directly · partial: some key facts, or a weaker version · silent: the page does not address it (also cookie walls, menus, junk) · contradicts: the page states something incompatible. Label alone. Do not discuss with your teammate until both of you have exported.

Claim check. Does the EXTRACTED CLAIM keep the main factual claim of the NOTE, without changing its meaning, strength (hedging) or key facts (names, numbers, dates)? Mark correct or incorrect, and say why if incorrect.

Passage task. The same four labels as the support task, but judge only the PASSAGE shown (a short window of the page), not the full page and not what you know. Label alone.
